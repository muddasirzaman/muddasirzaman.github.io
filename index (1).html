Biography

I am a final-year Biomedical Engineering student at Salim Habib University, Karachi. Internships at SMBBIT and PNS Shifa Hospital gave me practical exposure to how medical equipment is installed, inspected, maintained and repaired in clinical settings.

My most recent internship, at PCSIR, was research-focused: I contributed to evaluating large language models for diabetes education and to the GLYCO AI application, and supported a manuscript revision.

For my final year project I am building a handheld patient monitor with AI integration. Alongside it, I develop a patient-level evaluation pipeline for prostate cancer detection on MRI, under the guidance of Dr. Irfan Ahmed Usmani.
